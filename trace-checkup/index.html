Where to train, and what your log is enough for

A log of conversations and tool calls is enough to train by copying the examples that worked. Training by trial and reward also needs a way to score each try: every platform that offers it asks for one.

How to collect your log so it can answer

The eleven things a useful log records. The report marks each one recorded, partly recorded or missing.

Most often missing: what came back from each action, a task name, and whether it worked.

  • 1A number or name for each conversation, and the order of its steps
  • 2Every action: which tool, and what was sent to it
  • 3What came back from each action, exactly as it was, errors and empty answers too
  • 4A task name: the same name every time the same kind of job is done
  • 5Whether each conversation worked: yes or no, or a score from 0 to 1, and who decided it (an automatic check as it ran, or a person afterwards)
  • 6Everything the person said to the agent, the first message and any later ones
  • 7The same task tried at least 8 times, each marked worked or failed
  • 8The instructions (system prompt) and the list of tools the agent had in each conversation, as they were then, with what each tool needs
  • 9Which AI model (and version) ran the conversation
  • 10How many tokens each conversation used, input and output
  • 11Optional: a copy of the records the agent will work on, taken before it starts

How much

  • Each task tried 8 times, each marked worked or failed: then you can see how much trying again already fixes.
  • About 5,000 conversations that worked, each saying who decided it worked, before training. Below this, published results go both ways: training made some models better and some worse (MUA-RL, arXiv 2508.18669; ToolRL, arXiv 2504.13958). We can't tell which way yours would go.
  • Many different tasks tell you more than many copies of a few.

Private by design

  • It runs on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • As soon as the log is read, these are hidden: emails, most card numbers, common kinds of keys, and the first word after “password:”. Phone numbers are hidden only when written in common ways, such as +44 20 7946 0958, (415) 555-0132 or 13812345678 — not every one, so hide the others yourself. Names and addresses are not hidden.
  • It reads only the file you give it, and never changes it.

Questions

Will you train a model for us?

If you want: on the training platform you choose, with your own account (coming soon). We will still tell you when not to.

Do training platforms pay you?

No. We sell neither training nor computing power, and no platform pays us. That is why the report can tell you not to train.

How sure is the forecast?

It gives a direction and a range, never a single number. Each card says how sure it is, what it rests on and what would change it. With too little to go on, it says it can't tell.

What does “at the same cost” mean?

Letting the agent try a task again until it works takes more tries. The report counts on your log how many tries that takes on average, and weighs training against it: one try against that many. If your log records tokens, it also says how many tokens one try uses. Comparing in money on this site: coming soon.

When is trying again not an option?

When the agent can't tell, as it runs, that a try failed, or when its actions change records and aren't safe to do twice, such as a refund paid twice. The report asks you about both.

What does my log need to have?

Each conversation, every action the agent took and what came back, and ideally whether the task worked and who decided that. The report lists what is missing.

Who can see my log?

No one. It is read on this computer and not sent anywhere.
