What to collect

The eleven things a useful log records

The check-up marks each one recorded, partly recorded or missing. Start collecting them now, and your log is ready when you want to train.

Most often missing: what came back from each action, a task name, and whether it worked.

  1. 01

    A number or name for each conversation, and the order of its steps

  2. 02

    Every action: which tool, and what was sent to it

  3. 03

    What came back from each action, exactly as it was, errors and empty answers too

    Often missing
  4. 04

    A task name: the same name every time the same kind of job is done

    Often missing
  5. 05

    Whether each conversation worked: yes or no, or a score from 0 to 1, and who decided it (an automatic check as it ran, or a person afterwards)

    Often missing
  6. 06

    Everything the person said to the agent, the first message and any later ones

  7. 07

    The same task tried at least 8 times, each marked worked or failed

  8. 08

    The instructions (system prompt) and the list of tools the agent had in each conversation, as they were then, with what each tool needs

  9. 09

    Which AI model (and version) ran the conversation

  10. 10

    How many tokens each conversation used, input and output

  11. 11

    Optional: a copy of the records the agent will work on, taken before it starts

How much

  • 8tries per task

    Each task tried 8 times, each marked worked or failed: then you can see how much trying again already fixes.

  • 5,000conversations that worked

    About 5,000, each saying who decided it worked, before training. Below this, published results go both ways (MUA-RL, arXiv 2508.18669; ToolRL, arXiv 2504.13958).

  • Manydifferent tasks

    Many different tasks tell you more than many copies of a few.

Privacy

Your log stays on your computer.

Open source · code published when the first team asks

  • It runs on your computer

    Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

  • Only the files you give it

    It never changes them. Emails, most card numbers and common keys are hidden as soon as it reads the log; names and addresses are not.

  • You submit to your platform

    When the files are ready (coming soon), you send them with your own account. We never touch your keys.

  • Neutral

    No training platform pays us, so the advice doesn't depend on where you train.

Questions

Will you train a model for us?

Training runs on the platform you choose, with your own account. We get the data ready: what to send, a success check and the held-out tasks, as files you submit (coming soon). Then we help you check the trained model (also coming soon).

Do training platforms pay you?

No. We sell neither training nor computing power, and no platform pays us. So the advice doesn't depend on which platform you pick.

How sure is the forecast?

It gives a direction and a range, never a single number. Each card says how sure it is, what it rests on and what would change it. With too little to go on, it says it can't tell.

What does “at the same cost” mean?

Letting the agent try a task again until it works takes more tries. The report counts on your log how many tries that takes on average, and weighs training against it: one try against that many. Comparing in money in the report: coming soon.

When is trying again not an option?

When the agent can't tell, as it runs, that a try failed, or when its actions change records and aren't safe to do twice, such as a refund paid twice. The report asks you about both.

What does my log need to have?

Each conversation, every action the agent took and what came back, and ideally whether the task worked and who decided that. The report lists what is missing.

Who can see my log?

No one. It is read on this computer and not sent anywhere.
